FAQ

Quick answers

What does a revenue operations consultant do?

Aligns the systems and processes behind marketing, sales and customer success: funnel definitions, routing, data quality, integrations and reporting.

Is outsourced RevOps right for us?

Usually when the work is real but part time, covers several tools, or comes in bursts. Our guide on hiring vs outsourcing RevOps walks through the decision.

Do you also do marketing operations?

Yes. Lead scoring, nurture, campaign tracking and marketing automation in HubSpot, Marketo and Pardot are part of the same work.

How do you price it?

The audit call is free. Fix projects start from $900 and ongoing support from $500 a month, with a fixed quote before any work starts.
