Example projects

These are typical projects we take on, not specific client stories. Every engagement starts with an audit, so yours will be shaped around your setup.

Gainsight cleanup after an admin leaves

Typical starting point: A CS team of around 10 CSMs inherited a Gainsight instance nobody fully understands. Rules fail quietly, CTAs pile up and nobody trusts the health scores.

What we do:

  • Audit of rules, data jobs, CTAs and scorecards
  • Turn off or rebuild what is broken or unused
  • Simple documentation so the next person can run it

Typical timeline: 2 to 4 weeks

Health scores that predict churn

Typical starting point: Leadership wants to know which accounts are at risk before renewal, but today's scores are mostly based on gut feel.

What we do:

  • Pick the signals that matter: usage, support, engagement, sentiment
  • Build and weight the scorecard in Gainsight
  • Renewal and risk dashboard for CS leaders

Typical timeline: 3 to 5 weeks

Gainsight and CRM in sync

Typical starting point: Account owners, ARR and renewal dates in Gainsight don't match Salesforce or HubSpot, so CSMs keep checking two systems.

What we do:

  • Map fields and decide which system owns what
  • Fix the connector and rules that move data
  • Checks that flag mismatches before they spread

Typical timeline: 1 to 3 weeks

Frequently asked questions

What does a Gainsight consultant do?

A Gainsight consultant sets up, fixes and maintains your Gainsight instance. That usually means designing health scores, building CTAs and playbooks, connecting Gainsight to your CRM, cleaning up data and rules, and building the reports your CS leaders use for renewals and churn.

Do we need a full time Gainsight admin?

Many growing teams don't. If your instance is set up well, a few hours of admin help a month is often enough to handle changes, fix rules and keep data clean. We can be that support until you're big enough to hire someone full time.

Can you fix our existing Gainsight setup instead of starting over?

Yes, and that's usually the better option. We start with an audit of your current setup, keep what works, and fix the parts that cause bad data, noisy CTAs or health scores nobody trusts.

Do you work with Salesforce and HubSpot as well?

Yes. Most Gainsight problems start with the data coming in from the CRM, so we work on both sides of the sync. We set up and fix Salesforce and HubSpot as part of the same work.

How do you price Gainsight work?

We work on fixed price projects for setups and cleanups, or hourly for ongoing admin support. You get a clear quote after a free 30 minute audit call, before any work starts.
