Example projects

HubSpot portal cleanup

Typical starting point: A growing B2B team has years of old properties, overlapping workflows and thousands of duplicates. Nobody wants to touch anything in case it breaks.

What we do:

  • Full portal audit with a prioritised fix list
  • Merge duplicates and archive unused properties and lists
  • Consolidate workflows and document what each one does

Typical timeline: 2 to 4 weeks

Lifecycle stages sales and marketing agree on

Typical starting point: Marketing says leads are qualified, sales says they aren't, and the funnel report changes depending on who builds it.

What we do:

  • Workshop to agree MQL, SQL and opportunity rules
  • Build lifecycle and lead status automation in HubSpot
  • Funnel dashboard both teams use

Typical timeline: 2 to 3 weeks

Fast, fair lead routing

Typical starting point: Inbound demo requests sit unassigned for hours and sometimes go to the wrong rep.

What we do:

  • Routing rules by territory, segment or round robin
  • Enrichment so every lead has the data routing needs
  • Alerts in Slack or email so reps respond fast

Typical timeline: 1 to 3 weeks

Frequently asked questions

What does a HubSpot consultant do?

A HubSpot consultant sets up and fixes your HubSpot portal so it matches how your team markets and sells. That includes lifecycle stages, lead scoring, workflows, lead routing, integrations with other tools, data cleanup and reporting.

Can you work in our existing HubSpot portal?

Yes. Most of our work is fixing and improving portals that are already in use. We start with an audit, keep what works and clean up the rest, without disrupting your team's day to day work.

Do you work with HubSpot and Salesforce together?

Yes. Many teams run HubSpot for marketing and Salesforce for sales. We set up the sync, field mapping and rules so data flows the right way and stops overwriting itself.

Are you based in Toronto?

Yes. Afaao is based in Toronto and works remotely with B2B teams across the world, and we plan our working hours around your time zone.

How do you price HubSpot consulting?

We work on fixed price projects for setups and cleanups, or hourly for ongoing support. You get a clear quote after a free 30 minute audit call, before any work starts.
