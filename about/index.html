About Afaao

The ops team you don't have to hire.

Afaao is a Toronto based revenue operations consultancy. We set up, fix and connect the systems behind marketing, sales and customer success for growing B2B teams around the world.

Who we are

We are a small team of practitioners who have spent years inside B2B companies running marketing operations, revenue systems and customer success operations. We have been the people who get the message when leads stop routing, when the Salesforce sync breaks, or when leadership asks why two reports do not match.

Afaao exists because most growing teams have that same work and nobody whose job it is. You do not need a full time hire for it yet. You need someone who has done it before to fix it properly and keep it running.

What we work on

HubSpot, Salesforce and Gainsight are our core platforms. Around them we work across marketing automation, enrichment, routing, sales engagement, integrations and reporting. See the full list of tools, or the HubSpot, Salesforce and Gainsight pages.

Fit

Are we the right fit?

We're a good fit if

  • B2B teams of roughly 5 to 100 people
  • No full time ops person, or one who is stretched thin
  • A CRM that works but nobody fully trusts
  • A clear problem to fix, like routing, sync, reporting or a migration

We're probably not if

  • You need a full time, on site admin
  • You want strategy slides with no hands on build
  • You are looking for lead generation or ad management
