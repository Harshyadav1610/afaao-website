FAQ

Quick answers

What does a CRM consultant do?

Helps you choose, set up, clean up and connect your CRM so it matches how you sell, and keeps it healthy with documentation and admin support.

Which CRMs do you work with?

Mostly HubSpot and Salesforce, plus Pipedrive, Zoho CRM and Microsoft Dynamics 365, and migrations between them.

Should we use HubSpot or Salesforce?

It depends on your team and sales process. Our HubSpot vs Salesforce guide covers how to choose, and the free audit call gives you a straight answer for your setup.

How do you price it?

The audit call is free. Fix projects start from $900 and monthly support from $500 a month, with a fixed quote before any work starts.
