Pipedrive to HubSpot migration: a practical checklist

By the Afaao team · November 12, 2026 · 6 min read

Moving from Pipedrive to HubSpot looks simple on paper. Export, import, done. In practice the teams that struggle are the ones that moved everything as it was, including years of duplicate fields and half filled records. A migration is the best chance you will get to clean house, so it is worth doing in the right order.

Short answer

To migrate from Pipedrive to HubSpot, export and reconcile your Pipedrive data, decide which fields to keep, map them to HubSpot properties, and import in order: companies, contacts, deals, then activities. Save original create dates in a custom property, run a small test import first, and keep a master export as a backup.

Before you export anything

  • Agree on lifecycle stages and deal stages in HubSpot first. Pipedrive is built around deals, while HubSpot also tracks where every contact is in the funnel.
  • List every Pipedrive field and mark it keep, merge or drop. Most teams find a large share of custom fields are unused or duplicated.
  • Decide who owns the migration and who signs off on the mapping.

Step 1: export and reconcile

Export organisations, people, deals, activities and notes from Pipedrive. Check the row counts against what Pipedrive shows, and keep an untouched copy as your master sheet. You will come back to it more often than you expect.

Step 2: clean the data in the sheets

  • Remove obvious duplicates and test records.
  • Standardise values for fields like country, industry and lead source, so they match the dropdown options you will create in HubSpot.
  • Make sure every person has an email address where possible, since HubSpot uses email to deduplicate contacts.
  • Make sure every organisation has a website domain, since HubSpot uses domain to deduplicate companies.

Step 3: build the properties in HubSpot

Create the custom properties you decided to keep, with the right field types and dropdown options. Use HubSpot's default properties where one already exists instead of creating a copy. Build your pipelines and deal stages now too.

Step 4: keep your original dates

HubSpot sets a record's create date when it is imported, so every migrated record will look like it was created on migration day. Create custom date properties such as Original create date and Original close date, and map the Pipedrive dates into them. If you want lifecycle stage dates to reflect history, plan how you will set those as well. Skipping this step is the most common regret after a migration.

Step 5: import in the right order

  1. Companies first.
  2. Contacts next, associated to their companies.
  3. Deals after that, associated to contacts and companies.
  4. Activities and notes last, where your import method supports them.
  5. Owners need to exist as HubSpot users before you import, so records can be assigned.

Run a small test import of a few dozen records through every step before the full load. Check associations, owners, dates and dropdown values on the test records.

Step 6: check, then switch

  • Compare record counts by object and by owner against your master sheet.
  • Spot check a sample of deals with long histories.
  • Rebuild the few reports the team relies on and compare the numbers.
  • Set a cutover date, freeze changes in Pipedrive, and train the team on the new pipeline before day one.

What this looked like on a real migration

This checklist comes from doing it. One of our team led a Pipedrive to HubSpot migration in house at a B2B company, with more than 200,000 records to move.

Most of the time went into the steps before the import. The Pipedrive data was reconciled against the source, fields were rebuilt and standardised, redundant fields were removed, and lifecycle stages were added, since Pipedrive had no equivalent. Only then were the 200,000 plus records bulk imported into HubSpot.

About a year later the company added Salesforce with the HubSpot sync. The Pipedrive master sheet kept from the first migration turned out to be the most valuable file in the project. It was used to rebuild original create dates and lifecycle date stamps, and more than 200,000 contacts were loaded into Salesforce with the right history before the sync was switched on.

  • Lesson 1: cleanup and field decisions took far longer than the import. Plan for that.
  • Lesson 2: keep the untouched master export. It was still needed a year later.
  • Lesson 3: load history into the second system first, then turn on the sync.

If Salesforce is coming later

Some teams add Salesforce after moving to HubSpot. If that is on your roadmap, keep the master sheet and the original date properties. They let you load history into Salesforce with the right dates before you switch on the HubSpot and Salesforce sync, which avoids a flood of sync errors and duplicates.

FAQ

Quick answers

Can HubSpot import data directly from Pipedrive?

You can import Pipedrive exports as files, and there are migration tools and services that connect the two. Whichever route you choose, the cleanup, mapping and date decisions in this checklist still apply.

How long does a Pipedrive to HubSpot migration take?

It depends on the number of records, custom fields and how much cleanup is needed. The planning and cleanup usually take longer than the import itself.
