HubSpot vs Salesforce for small B2B teams: how to choose

By the Afaao team · November 23, 2026 · 7 min read

HubSpot vs Salesforce is one of the most argued questions in B2B. The honest answer is that both are good, and most teams that struggle with either one have a setup problem, not a software problem. What matters is which one fits the team you have today and the one you will have in two years.

Part of our CRM consulting guides.

Short answer

For most B2B teams under about 50 people, HubSpot is the easier choice: faster to set up, easier for marketing and sales to share, and simpler to run without a full time admin. Salesforce fits better when you need complex sales processes, heavy customisation or many teams and territories, and you have someone to own it.

The short version

HubSpot is built to be usable out of the box by marketing and sales together. Salesforce is built to be shaped into almost anything, which is powerful but needs someone to do the shaping. If you have no admin and want marketing and sales in one place, HubSpot usually wins. If your sales process is complex and you have or will hire someone to own the CRM, Salesforce often wins. Our CRM consulting work starts with exactly this question.

Setup and day to day admin

  • HubSpot: most small teams can be up and running in weeks. Lifecycle stages, forms, email and pipelines are connected from the start.
  • Salesforce: more decisions up front about objects, fields, page layouts, permissions and automation. Great results, but rarely without an admin or a partner.
  • Ongoing effort: HubSpot can run with a few hours a month of admin time for a small team. Salesforce usually needs more, because every customisation needs looking after.

Marketing and sales together

HubSpot keeps marketing automation and the CRM in one system, so contacts, emails, forms and deals share one record. Salesforce teams usually add a separate marketing tool such as Account Engagement (Pardot), Marketo or HubSpot itself, and then manage a sync. That works, but it is one more thing to keep healthy, which is why we wrote a full guide to fixing HubSpot and Salesforce sync issues.

Flexibility and complex sales

Salesforce shines when you have several sales teams, territories, complex approvals, partner channels, custom objects everywhere or industry specific needs. HubSpot has closed much of the gap with custom objects and more advanced automation on higher tiers, but very complex processes still tend to feel more natural in Salesforce.

Reporting

Both can produce the reports leadership needs. HubSpot reporting is quicker to build for marketing and funnel questions. Salesforce reporting is very flexible for sales operations, especially once the data model is clean. In both, bad definitions break reports faster than any tool limit, so agree on your HubSpot lifecycle stages or Salesforce stages and what counts as an MQL versus an SQL first.

Cost, honestly

List prices change often and both vendors discount, so compare a real quote for your seats and features. Remember the hidden cost: the people time to run it. A cheaper licence that needs a full time admin can cost more than a pricier one your team can run itself.

When it makes sense to run both

Many growing companies use HubSpot for marketing and Salesforce for sales. It is a solid setup when each team strongly prefers its tool and someone owns the sync. If nobody owns it, it becomes a constant source of duplicates and overwritten fields. If you already run both, our Salesforce consultants and HubSpot consulting teams work on that sync every week.

A quick way to decide

  1. Do you have, or plan to hire, someone whose job is the CRM? If not, lean HubSpot.
  2. Is your sales process simple enough to draw on one page? If yes, lean HubSpot. If it needs several pages, lean Salesforce.
  3. Does marketing need deep automation tied to the same records sales uses? Lean HubSpot.
  4. Do you need territories, complex approvals or heavy custom objects now? Lean Salesforce.
  5. Are you switching from something else? Read our Pipedrive to HubSpot migration checklist before you commit to a date.
FAQ

Quick answers

Is HubSpot or Salesforce better for small businesses?

For most small B2B teams without a dedicated admin, HubSpot is easier to set up and run. Salesforce suits teams with complex sales processes and someone to own the CRM.

Can you use HubSpot and Salesforce together?

Yes. Many teams run HubSpot for marketing and Salesforce for sales with the native integration. It works well when someone owns the sync and field rules.
