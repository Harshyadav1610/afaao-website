HubSpot connector for Claude and ChatGPT: what it can read, what it can change, and why access gets blocked

By the Afaao team · November 18, 2026 · 6 min read

Connecting Claude or ChatGPT to HubSpot takes a few clicks. Then someone asks it to update a deal and the answer is no. The HubSpot Community has had a steady run of threads about this lately, from write access that stays blocked after a reconnect to people asking what the connector can see at all. Here is what HubSpot's own documentation says each connector can do, and a checklist for when it won't.

Part of our HubSpot consulting guides.

Short answer

Both connectors read most of your HubSpot data and can create and update records, but never delete, and they only reach what your own HubSpot user can reach. Blocked access usually comes from one of five things: no Super Admin approval, write permissions left off, no reconnect after a change, your user permissions, or Sensitive Data settings.

What the connector is

HubSpot offers one connector for Claude and one for ChatGPT. Both let you ask questions about your HubSpot data in plain language from the chat window and, if you allow it, create and update records from there too. Both are available on all HubSpot products and plans. On the Claude side, HubSpot's article says you also need a paid Claude plan. Setup is the same idea in both apps: you open the connector settings in the AI app, sign in to HubSpot, pick the account, choose the permissions and click Connect app.

An AI connector is only as good as the data under it. If records are messy, start with the free HubSpot audit checklist, or talk to our HubSpot consulting team.

What they can read and change

HubSpot publishes a full table for each connector. The short version, as of November 2026:

  • Read, create and update: contacts, leads, companies, deals, tickets, custom objects, line items, products, pipelines, and calls, meetings, notes, tasks and emails.
  • Read, create and update on the marketing side: landing pages, website pages, blog posts, marketing emails and segments.
  • Read and create, but not update: marketing events and properties. You can add a new property, but not edit an existing one.
  • Read only: invoices, orders, carts, subscriptions, inbox and help desk conversations, users, teams and saved reports.
  • Delete: not supported for any object, in either connector.

There are a few differences between the two. Quotes are read only in ChatGPT, while the Claude connector has quote creation and updates in beta. Campaigns can be updated through ChatGPT, but only read and created through Claude. Workflows and sequences are not on either list, so expect a written plan you build yourself rather than a finished workflow. For pages and blog posts, only published content comes back in full. Drafts return basic details only.

Limits worth knowing before you let it write

  • It creates or updates at most 10 records per request. Big cleanups still belong in imports or workflows.
  • Your validation rules may not protect you. HubSpot's Claude article says pipeline stage validation and conditional property rules apply, but association label validations do not. The ChatGPT article says custom validation rules, including pipeline stage and association label validations, are not applied. So a rule that stops a rep inside HubSpot may not stop the chat.
  • All use is subject to HubSpot's API usage limits.
  • Only one HubSpot account can be connected to a Claude or ChatGPT account at a time. If you work across several portals, you disconnect and reconnect to switch.
  • Every create and update shows in the HubSpot audit log, attributed to both the user and the connector.

Why access gets blocked

Most blocked requests come down to five causes. Work through them in this order, since the first ones are the quickest to rule out.

  1. The connector was never approved for you. Super Admins and users with App Marketplace Access can connect without approval. Everyone else needs a Super Admin to approve the connector first. Until then, HubSpot tells the user they are not authorized and to ask their Super Admin for access.
  2. Write permissions were left off. When a Super Admin approves the connector, they choose which optional data permissions to turn on, and that choice applies to every user. If write access or engagements were not switched on, the connector can read but cannot change anything.
  3. Nobody reconnected after a change. When a Super Admin updates the connector's data permissions, each user has to disconnect and connect again to pick them up. HubSpot also notes that new permissions can take up to two hours to appear after reconnecting.
  4. Your own HubSpot permissions say no. The connector only reaches the data your user can reach, and custom restrictions carry over. If you cannot see or edit a record, a property or a pipeline by hand, the chat cannot either.
  5. Sensitive Data is turned on. In accounts with Sensitive Data settings on, the connectors cannot update engagements, and depending on how the account is set up they may not see them at all. Read access to sensitive properties is an Enterprise option a Super Admin has to set up, and Highly Sensitive Data properties stay restricted either way.

Several Community posts this month describe the same pattern. The connector reports that read access works but write access needs a permission change or a reauthorization, and the user has already reconnected once. The answers in those threads point back to the causes above, most often the second and third.

A ten minute check for blocked write access

  1. Make the same edit by hand in HubSpot on one record. If HubSpot blocks you, fix your user permissions or the property's edit rules first. The connector will not get around them.
  2. Ask a Super Admin to open Settings, then Integrations, then Connected Apps, and check the Approved apps tab. Confirm the connector is approved, who is allowed to install it, and which optional data permissions are on.
  3. Disconnect HubSpot inside Claude or ChatGPT, connect again, and select the write permissions on the permissions page before you click Connect app.
  4. If a plain reconnect does not pick up the change, disconnect on both sides. Remove it in the AI app, then in HubSpot open Connected Apps, click the connector, and use the Actions menu to uninstall it. Then connect again from the AI app.
  5. In Claude or ChatGPT, set the HubSpot write tools to Needs approval. HubSpot recommends this over Always allow, so every change is shown to you first and only goes through when you type Yes.
  6. Run one small test, like updating one field on one test record, and confirm it.
  7. Open the HubSpot audit log and check that the change landed and who it is attributed to.
  8. If it still fails after all of that, contact HubSpot support with the record ID and the exact message you saw.

Before you switch it on for the whole team

  • Decide who should be able to install it. HubSpot lets a Super Admin limit installs to admins, to specific users and teams, or open it to everyone.
  • List the properties the connector should never change, and protect them with HubSpot field level permissions where your plan has them, since validation rules may not stop the chat.
  • Start with a week of read only questions. Pipeline summaries, deals with no next step and open tickets by owner are good first prompts, and they show quickly whether the answers match your reports.
  • Remember that agencies and consultants with Super Admin or App Marketplace access can connect too. Review who has connected in the audit log.
  • Keep the data clean. The connector answers from what is in HubSpot, so duplicates, missing owners and empty fields show up in its answers.

When it is not the connector

If the permissions check out and the answers still look wrong, the problem is usually the data underneath: duplicate companies, contacts with no owner, lifecycle stages nobody maintains. That is the same cleanup you would do before any reporting project, and our guide to fixing a messy HubSpot portal walks through the order to do it in. Get that right first and the connector becomes a lot more useful, because it can only be as accurate as the records it reads.

FAQ

Quick answers

Can the HubSpot connector for Claude or ChatGPT delete records?

No. HubSpot's documentation says delete is not supported for any object in either connector. It can create and update supported records, up to 10 at a time.

Do I need a paid HubSpot plan to use the connector?

No. HubSpot lists both connectors as available on all HubSpot products and plans. For Claude, HubSpot's article says you need a paid Claude plan.

Why can Claude read my deals but not update them?

Usually because write permissions were not approved, or you have not reconnected since they were, or because your own HubSpot user cannot edit those deals. Check those in that order, then test one record by hand.
